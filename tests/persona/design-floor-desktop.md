# The chess floor holds up to a designer's review at desktop width

## Goal
Review the chess floor page at the full window width, top to bottom, with a game on. Then open one move and review that page too. The position changes about once a minute while a game is on. If what you were looking at changes under you, carry on with the new position; that is normal here.

## Passes when
- the board is unmistakably the main thing on the page and sits where the eye lands
- the columns beside the board are balanced and nothing shifts the board when their content changes
- type sizes, weights and spacing are consistent between the board's surroundings and the rest of the page
- the arrows' shading reads as a ranking without a legend
- no block of multi-line text is centre-aligned
- every state you saw (bot to move, opponent to move, a move being decided) looked designed, not left over
