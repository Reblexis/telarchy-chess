# A visitor can replay a finished game and understand what they are watching

## Goal
Find a way to watch one of the bot's earlier games on this page. Play it, pause it, drag through it, then get back to the live game.

## Passes when
- you found the replay controls without being told where they are
- you could pick a particular past game and tell from the list who it was against and how it ended
- while it played you understood the line that describes each move, including what "chosen at" a number means
- you could tell at all times whether you were looking at a replay or at the live game
- getting back to the live game took one obvious action
